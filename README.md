## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a Computer Science Engineering student focused on **AI/ML, Computer Vision, and applied data analysis** — building systems that turn raw data and pixels into working, real-world applications rather than just notebooks.

My interest sits at the intersection of **software engineering fundamentals** and **machine learning practice**: writing clean, maintainable Python, understanding the math behind the models I use, and shipping projects that behave predictably outside of a training notebook. I care about the full pipeline — data cleaning, model selection, evaluation, and presenting results in a way a non-technical stakeholder could actually use.
